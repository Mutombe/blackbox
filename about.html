Supplying Zimbabwe’s makers since 2014

Blackbox Investments initiated trading in Zimbabwe in 2014. The goal was, and still is, to supply needed raw materials to manufacturing companies.

Who we are

We are a Zimbabwean trading company built around a single, unglamorous promise: the material you specified, in the quantity you ordered, on the day we said it would arrive.

Blackbox Investments started trading in 2014 to close a gap every local manufacturer knows. Inputs arrive late. They arrive short. Sometimes they arrive as something quietly different from what was ordered. Any one of those costs a production line far more than the material is worth.

The company has a work ethic and principle established on aggressive sales and marketing. In practice that means a team which chases supply as hard as it chases orders. We would rather ring you early with a problem than let a delivery date slip past. That is what we mean by a vibrant and motivated approach to serving our expanding customer base.
